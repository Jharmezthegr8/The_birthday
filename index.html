I hope this is only one of the many birthdays we’ll celebrate together. I hope we continue to grow alongside each other, witness each other’s milestones, laugh at the most ridiculous things, have our occasional disagreements, make up and create even more beautiful memories together.

Thank you for being a part of my life and for sharing this portion of life with me. No matter how much life changes us or where our individual journeys take us, I hope there will always be a place for our friendship in the lives we’re building.

Happy birthday, my person🥹❤️ Here’s to more life, more laughter, more memories and many more years of doing life together. I love you, and I’m genuinely grateful our paths crossed🫂❤️🎂
